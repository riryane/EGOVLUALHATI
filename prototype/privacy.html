Privacy Notice

This notice explains how the eTulong service processes your personal information, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

What we collect

  • Identity data from your eGovPH account: name, birth date, sex, nationality, photo, and PhilSys record
  • Contact details: mobile number, email, and address
  • Application records: programs applied for, documents you submit, decisions, and claim confirmations

Why we process it

  • To determine your eligibility for social assistance programs
  • To process, review, and release ayuda applications
  • To verify your identity when claiming benefits (SMS codes, face verification)
  • To notify you about your application status

Legal basis

Processing is necessary for the delivery of government services under the mandates of the implementing agencies (DSWD, DOLE, CHED) and your consent given upon first login.

Retention

Application records are retained for the period required by government auditing rules, after which they are securely disposed of.

Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed
  • Access and obtain a copy of your data
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information (through eGovPH)
  • Object to or withdraw consent from processing
  • Request erasure where legally permissible
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph)
